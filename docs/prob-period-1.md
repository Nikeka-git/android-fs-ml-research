## 7. Current project status, limitations and next steps

The intended goal is to use ML/AI to select an existing exploit most suitable for an Android device's characteristics. The repository contains two modules: `binder-diagnostics`, a diagnostic and simulation project snapshot, and `android-poc-research`, a consolidated snapshot of existing PoC source files and research reports. Completed materials include the module catalog, provenance records, 204-file integrity index, 12-source diagnostic-text registry, data-preparation rules, evaluation methodology, analytical summary, repository navigation and updated task-board evidence.

An implemented and trained Android exploit-selection model, its training dataset and verified selection-quality results are not evidenced in this snapshot. The 12-text registry supports diagnostic research; its annotations still need independent review and its actual split remains unassigned. The joint Assignment 3 reports text-prototype results, but the corresponding code, outputs and models have not been supplied or rerun here. Importing PoC materials did not verify their runtime behaviour. Existing JUnit results record 24 tests with no failures or errors; the stored lint report records no errors and one warning. These reports were preserved, not rerun. Formal independent reviews and the complete updated general report remain outstanding.

Next steps are to independently review annotations, obtain the text-prototype programs and outputs, fix a defensible data split, and agree the module-specific research questions, test environment and evaluation protocol. Subsequent implementation and assessment should preserve actual revisions, dates, contributors, unsuccessful observations and review evidence. Complete the general report and outstanding submission checks against the official requirements.
